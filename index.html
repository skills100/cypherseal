A zero-trust vault for academic records and private files

CipherSeal Vault protects transcripts, result sheets, theses and personal messages with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Keys are derived from a long random password with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 using a dynamic iteration count that rises with the sensitivity of the record. Every decryption is verified, wrong guesses are limited, and encrypted items can disappear after a timer or after a single view. It is free, needs no account, and runs entirely in your browser.

Camera guard required

This is a protected item. Allow camera access and keep your face in view with no phone in sight. The camera is analyzed on this device only; no photos or video are recorded or uploaded. If a phone, a second person or a covered lens is detected, the content is hidden at once and the session ends.

Your camera stays off until you choose to enable it.

A strong deterrent, not a guarantee: it sees only what this camera sees, so a phone out of frame or a second camera elsewhere cannot be detected. Nothing in a browser can block OS screenshots or screen recording.
