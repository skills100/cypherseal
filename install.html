Install the CipherSeal app

Protected, view-once and self-destruct items open only in the CipherSeal app. The app makes screenshots and screen recordings come out blank, which a web browser cannot do. Ordinary encrypted items still open in any browser.

Android

Download CipherSeal.apk

  1. Open the downloaded file (notification bar or the Downloads app).
  2. Android may say your browser is not allowed to install apps. Tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  3. If Google Play Protect says “App not scanned” or warns about an unrecognised app, tap More details, then Install anyway. This is normal: the app is not on the Play Store yet.
  4. Open CipherSeal, pick your encrypted file, enter the password, and allow the camera when asked.

You can switch “Allow from this source” off again afterwards.

iPhone and iPad

Apple does not allow installing apps from a web link unless the app is on the App Store or TestFlight. Until the project is published, the app is installed by the project team with one of these methods. All use a free Apple ID. The app stops opening after 7 days and must be re-installed or refreshed; this is an Apple rule for free accounts.

Add to AltStore (iPhone, needs AltStore) Download CipherSeal.ipa

Method 1: Sideloadly (Windows or Mac, with a cable)

  1. On the computer install Sideloadly from sideloadly.io (on a Mac: open the .dmg and drag it to Applications; on Windows also install iTunes and iCloud from Apple’s website, not the Microsoft Store versions).
  2. Download CipherSeal.ipa with the button above, on the computer.
  3. Plug the iPhone in by cable and tap Trust on the phone.
  4. Drag the .ipa into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, press Start.
  5. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple ID, tap Trust.
  6. iOS 16 or newer: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode, switch on and restart.
  7. Open CipherSeal.

Method 2: AltStore (refreshes itself over Wi-Fi)

  1. On the computer install AltServer from altstore.io, then use it to install AltStore onto the iPhone with a cable (same Apple ID).
  2. On the iPhone, open this page in Safari and tap Add to AltStore above. AltStore opens with the CipherSeal source; tap Free / Install.
  3. Keep AltServer running on the same Wi-Fi so AltStore can renew the app every 7 days.

Method 3: Xcode (Mac only)

  1. Run ./setup-ios.sh yourdomain.com in the native folder, open native/ios/App/App.xcworkspace, choose your iPhone, set Signing → Team to your Apple ID and press Run.

After launch, with a paid Apple Developer account, this becomes a normal App Store or TestFlight install with no 7-day limit.

Computers

There is no desktop app yet. Open protected items on an Android phone or iPhone with the app. Standard encrypted items open in any browser.
